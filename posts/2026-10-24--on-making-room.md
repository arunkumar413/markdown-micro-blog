## On making room

I've been thinking about the spaces between things. The walk before work. The pause before opening another tab. The quiet ten minutes when there isn't anything in particular to do.

> Almost everything will work again if you unplug it for a few minutes, including you.

There's a temptation to fill every gap. A podcast for the walk, a feed for the queue, another task for the spare half hour. But lately, I'm trying something different: leaving a little room.

### A small experiment

For the past week, I've been taking my morning coffee outside without my phone. No grand plan. Just coffee, the changing light, and whatever happens to be on my mind.

The first day felt strangely difficult. By the third, I noticed a bird building a nest above the gate. By the fifth, I had an idea for a project I'd been stuck on for a month.

I'm not sure there's a lesson here beyond this: some thoughts need a bit of empty space to arrive.

- Take a walk with no destination.
- Leave a page in the notebook blank.
- Let a question sit for a day.

Maybe making room is a kind of making, too.
